ABOUT THIS SERVICE

What is Digital X-Ray?

The requested area is positioned between the X-ray source and the digital detector. The technologist may ask you to hold still or briefly hold your breath to help obtain clear images.

COMMON REASONS

When may it be used?

  • Imaging a specific area when requested by a clinician
  • Assessing bones and joints after injury or for other concerns
  • Providing images that can be reviewed with examination findings
WHY IT CAN HELP

Potential benefits

  • Digital images are available for clinical review.
  • The examination is focused on the area requested.
  • Many routine X-ray exams are completed in a short visit.
YOUR APPOINTMENT

What happens?

A radiographer positions you for the requested view and takes the image. You may need to remove metal objects or change clothing if they overlap the area being imaged.

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

How to prepare

Most X-ray exams need little preparation. Tell the staff if you may be pregnant, and follow any specific instructions for your examination.

Is an X-ray painful?

Taking an image is generally painless, although holding a position may feel uncomfortable if you have an injury. Tell the technologist so they can help you position safely.
