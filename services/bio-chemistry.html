ABOUT THIS SERVICE

What is Bio-Chemistry?

A clinician may request one test or a group of tests. The exact panel depends on your health history, symptoms or monitoring needs; a result is interpreted alongside other clinical information.

COMMON REASONS

When may it be used?

  • Routine health screening when advised
  • Assessing selected organ and metabolic functions
  • Monitoring a known condition or response to care
WHY IT CAN HELP

Potential benefits

  • Test selection can be tailored to the clinician&rsquo;s question.
  • Results may be compared with earlier tests when appropriate.
  • One visit may support collection for multiple ordered tests.
YOUR APPOINTMENT

What happens?

A trained staff member collects the required sample, usually blood or urine depending on the order. The laboratory processes it using the method specified for each test.

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

How to prepare

Some biochemistry tests require fasting or a particular collection time; others do not. Follow the instructions for your exact test and do not change prescribed medicines unless your clinician tells you to.

Can I eat before a biochemistry test?

It depends on the tests ordered. Check your test instructions in advance because some tests may require fasting.
