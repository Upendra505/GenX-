ABOUT THIS SERVICE

What is Pathology?

Pathology services examine cells, tissues and body fluids using appropriate laboratory methods. The specific test depends on the question your clinician is investigating.

COMMON REASONS

When may it be used?

  • Routine health checks and monitoring
  • Investigating symptoms alongside a clinical assessment
  • Following changes over time when repeat testing is advised
WHY IT CAN HELP

Potential benefits

  • A broad range of tests can be coordinated in one diagnostic centre.
  • Clear sample identification and organized laboratory handling.
  • Reports can help your clinician decide whether further evaluation is needed.
YOUR APPOINTMENT

What happens?

After confirming the requested tests, a sample is collected or received according to the test requirements. Some tests use blood; others use urine or a different sample. Your report should be reviewed with the clinician who requested it.

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

How to prepare

Preparation varies by test. Fasting, timing, medicines and sample collection instructions can affect some results, so follow the instructions given for your specific test.

Does every test require fasting?

No. Fasting depends on the particular test. Please confirm the instructions for your order before visiting.
