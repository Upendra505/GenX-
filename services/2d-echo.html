ABOUT THIS SERVICE

What is 2D Echo?

The scan uses sound waves to show heart structures and movement. A clinician interprets the images alongside symptoms, examination findings and other tests.

COMMON REASONS

When may it be used?

  • Reviewing heart structure and pumping motion when requested
  • Assessing selected heart concerns as part of clinical evaluation
  • Following up when a clinician recommends repeat imaging
WHY IT CAN HELP

Potential benefits

  • Provides moving images of the heart during the examination.
  • Uses ultrasound rather than X-ray radiation.
  • Can add imaging information to a broader cardiac assessment.
YOUR APPOINTMENT

What happens?

You lie on an examination couch while gel and an ultrasound probe are used on the chest. The sonographer may change your position to obtain different views.

BEFORE YOUR VISIT

How to prepare

A standard transthoracic echo often has no special preparation, but follow the instructions given for your appointment and bring any relevant referral details.

Is a 2D Echo the same as an ECG?

No. An echo uses ultrasound to create images of the heart. An ECG records the heart’s electrical activity; a clinician may request one or both tests.
